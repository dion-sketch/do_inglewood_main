Bundled with certification

Get certified. Get on the map. Same day.

Every Inglewood business already pays a city business tax — a minimum of about $93.50 a year — just to be registered. That's required either way, and today it's a line item that does nothing for them. Fold Do Inglewood into that same certification path: the moment a business becomes official with the City, it's automatically placed on the map, into the visitor app, and onto the list to be seen. The City's program gains a modern storefront; the business gets premium visibility it could never afford alone.

For the business
City-backed visibility, a verified badge, and real customers — the day they're licensed. Far more reach than $60 buys on the open market.
For the City
More businesses registering, paying taxes, and hiring locally — plus a live directory that keeps visitor spend inside Inglewood.
